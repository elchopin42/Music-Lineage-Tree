# Music-Lineage-Tree
Interactive database exploring the pedagogical connections and lineage between composers and musicians through history.

- All names of composers and cities are in their original language.
- For names and places originally in Cyrillic, Arabic, or other non-Latin alphabets, English transliteration has been used.
- Flags refer to the current country of each city rather than the historical state or empire to which it belonged at the time.
- In the case of disputed territories, the flag of the country currently in de facto control of the area has been used.
- Dates of birth and death for Russian composers may not be exact due to the historical use of the Julian calendar.
- This project only includes composers who passed away before the year 2026. Therefore, any deaths occurring after this date may not be reflected in the current data.
